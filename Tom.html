TOM HOLLAND

Profile
Name: Thomas Stanley Holland.
Date of Birth: June 1, 1996.
Age: 30 years old.
Birthplace: Kingston upon Thames, London, England.
Profession: Actor.
Major Film Roles:
Peter Parker / Spider-Man: Starred as the Marvel Cinematic Universe superhero across three standalone films (2017–2021) and multiple crossover movies.
Lucas Bennett: A young survivor of the 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami in the disaster drama The Impossible (2012).
Nathan Drake: The young treasure hunter and fortune seeker in the action-adventure movie Uncharted (2022).
Arvin Russell: A troubled young man protecting his family in the psychological thriller The Devil All the Time (2020).
Danny Sullivan: A young man arrested for a shooting with a fragmented mind in the psychological thriller series The Crowded Room (2023).
