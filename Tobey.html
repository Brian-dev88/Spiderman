TOBEY MAGUIRE

Profile
Name: Tobias Vincent Maguire.
Date of Birth: June 27, 1975.
Age: 51 years old.
Birthplace: Santa Monica, California, U.S.
Profession: Actor and film producer.
Major Film Roles:
Peter Parker / Spider-Man: Starred in Sam Raimi's Spider-Man trilogy (2002–2007) and reprised the character in Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021).
Red Pollard: The jockey in the Oscar-nominated sports drama Seabiscuit (2003).
Sam Cahill: A captive soldier dealing with severe PTSD in Brothers (2009).
Nick Carraway: The narrator and friend of Gatsby in The Great Gatsby (2013).
Homer Wells: The lead character in the drama The Cider House Rules
